**AwesomeB** introduces a new way to manage tabs: before closing them, it goes through the **suspend** state. Desktops organize tabs according to your workflow, and suspending them lets you keep them in their place and recover them later.

![Tab state](../../../assets/close-tab-1.png)

In the screenshot above, you can see a suspended tab (1) and two active ones (2 and 3).

There are several ways to close or suspend a tab:

## Suspend

<FeatureAccess entity="suspend a tab" shortcut="suspendTab" command="suspendTab" menu="tabs.suspend" />

Also, when a website is active and you hover over it, a button with a minus sign will appear, which means **suspend it**.

![Suspend a website](../../../assets/close-tab-2.png)

## Close

<FeatureAccess entity="close a tab" shortcut="closeTab" command="closeTab" menu="tabs.close"/>

Also, when a website is suspended and you hover over it, an X button appears, which means close it.

![Close a website](../../../assets/close-tab-3.png)

:::note
Keep in mind that when a tab is closed, it remains hidden for a few days ([see the configuration](/config/general/#closed-tabs-retention)) and can be recovered.
:::

:::note
Tabs opened with the **private** profile, when closed, disappear permanently. They cannot be reopened.
:::

## Context menu

Using the context menu on a tab, in addition to letting you suspend it or close it directly, you can also close all tabs below the current one.

![Context menu](../../../assets/close-tab-4.png)
