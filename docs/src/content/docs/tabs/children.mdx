export const menu = await getMenuItems('en', 'contextTab.openTargetBlankAsChild')
export const move = await getMenuItems('en', 'contextTab.move')

Imagine the following scenario.

In the *sidebar* you have three tabs: Jira, Tab 2, Tab 3. Jira opens all tickets as `target="_blank"`. This means new tabs will be created at the end, after Tab 3.

This is very inconvenient because the tabs end up disorganized, and that's why the following feature exists.

Open the context menu and select the option **{menu[0]}**.

![Enable child tabs](../../../assets/tab-child-1.jpeg)

From now on, all websites that open from this tab will become children.

![Child tabs example](../../../assets/tab-child-2.png)

This lets you move the parent up or down while keeping the children linked.

You can also use the context menu to close all children or collapse them.

![Close all children](../../../assets/tab-child-3.png)

When collapsed, you'll see a bracket in the sidebar, drawn around the children:

![Collapsed children](../../../assets/tab-child-4.png)

The bracket is made of two lines: a vertical one on the left and a horizontal one at the bottom, joined by a rounded corner. The horizontal line reaches the right margin of the sidebar, and the corner is rounded more softly than the tabs, so the bracket reads as one shape with the tab block. Click any part of it to expand the children again without going through the context menu. When the children are expanded the same bracket surrounds them, and clicking it collapses them back.

The bracket stays subtle at rest and only becomes fully opaque and slightly thicker when you hover over it, so the action is visible only when you need it.

If you want to detach a child from its parent and turn it into a regular top-level tab container, open the context menu on the child, choose **{move[0]}** and then *Move to top level*. The child keeps its own tabs and any children it has.

You can also drag a tab and drop it onto a tab with this option enabled to turn it into one of its children. See [move a tab](/tabs/move/#drag-and-drop).
