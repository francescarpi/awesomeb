You can move it to the right or to the left within the desktop list. The numbering updates automatically to maintain the correlative order.

## Drag and drop

You can also drag a desktop onto another one to swap their positions. The desktop you're dragging follows the pointer as a circle, and the desktop you drop it on highlights to show where it will land.

The numbering updates automatically to maintain the correlative order.
