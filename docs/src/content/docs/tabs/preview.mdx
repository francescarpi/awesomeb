---
title: Preview a website
---

While you browse a website and find a link, you'll often want to preview it before opening it as a new tab.

To preview it:

* macOS: `Opt+<click>`

![Link preview](../../../assets/tab-preview.png)

A new view will appear inside the same tab with the website's preview (1) so you can navigate and decide whether to dismiss it. You'll see that below the tab's title, an eye icon (3) is shown to indicate that tab has a preview open.

You have three available actions (2):

* Click the cross or press `esc` to dismiss it.
* Click the maximize arrows or press `enter` to open it as a new tab.
* Click the split icon to open the link in split view.
