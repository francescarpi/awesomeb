Hi! My name is Francesc Arpí, I was born in 1978 and I live in a village on the Costa Brava.

I remember perfectly the moment I fell in love with programming. I was 12, and a cousin of mine taught me, using BASIC, how to open a file, write to it, and read its content. That fascinated me! I immediately understood that, with that programming language, I could create anything that came to my mind. From that moment on, I couldn't stop programming, learning, experimenting with various languages, and creating.

Nowadays, this world still excites me, and although it's changing a lot due to AI, I can't see myself doing anything else.
